🩺 Adolescent Psychosocial Standard

The HEEADSSS Framework

A comprehensive clinical screening tool standard evaluating key psychosocial risk areas in adolescent health & well-being.

H

Home Environment

Family & Living

Assesses family dynamics, housing stability, safety, and communication with parents or guardians.

Focus: Conflict resolution, family support, living arrangements
E

Education & School

Academic Pressure

Evaluates academic performance, exam stress, attendance, school environment, and future career goals.

Focus: Academic strain, career anxiety, attendance
E

Eating & Nutrition

Body Image & Habits

Examines dietary habits, body image perception, weight concerns, and overall nutritional well-being.

Focus: Body positivity, meal regularity, healthy habits
A

Activities & Peers

Social Life & Hobbies

Explores friend groups, screen time, sports, hobbies, and social connectedness with peers.

Focus: Friendship quality, screen balance, hobbies
D

Drugs & Alcohol

Substance Exposure

Screens for exposure to tobacco, vaping, alcohol, or substance use among peers and self.

Focus: Peer influence awareness, healthy choices
S

Sexuality & Identity

Personal Identity

Addresses sexual health, relationship dynamics, self-identity, and gender or orientation comfort.

Focus: Relationship safety, identity support, boundaries
S

Suicide & Mood

Emotional Regulation

Identifies depression symptoms, anxiety, self-harm risks, hopelessness, and emotional crises.

Focus: Mood tracking, crisis prevention, mental health
S

Safety & Bullying

Security & Protection

Assesses online cyberbullying, physical safety at school/home, and exposure to violence.

Focus: Cyber safety, anti-bullying, safe environments
